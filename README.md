# Awesome-Field-Service-Construction

# Top Field Service Construction Platforms Ecosystem
**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**
*Focused on Jobsite Productivity, Plan Viewing, Daily Reports, Punch Lists, Field Tasks & Construction Workforce Coordination*
**Last updated: September 2026**

This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Field Service Construction** (construction field productivity and site management). These systems help general and specialty contractors manage plans, tasks, daily reports, punch lists, time tracking, safety, and real-time jobsite coordination.

**Examples** include Fieldwire, Raken, Procore Field Productivity, OpenSpace, HammerTech, Rhumbix, Touchplan, SiteMax, Assignar, and Fluix (the category leaders).

**Open-source emphasis**: Construction-specific field platforms are predominantly commercial. Practical open options include generic project management tools (**OpenProject**), ERP construction modules (**ERPNext**, **Odoo**), and experimental field-management prototypes. This section lists the strongest available open resources and is realistic about the commercial gap for mobile jobsite UX and industry workflows.

Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms
- **[Fieldwire (by Hilti)](https://www.fieldwire.com/)**  
  Field-first construction management platform focused on plan viewing, task assignment, punch lists, and daily coordination for crews on site.

- **[Raken](https://www.rakenapp.com/)**  
  Daily reporting, time tracking, and production tracking specialist built for construction field teams and supervisors.

- **[Procore Field Productivity](https://www.procore.com/)**  
  Field productivity capabilities within the broader Procore construction management platform—drawings, tasks, daily logs, and jobsite collaboration at enterprise scale.

- **[OpenSpace](https://www.openspace.ai/)**  
  Reality-capture and visual documentation platform that creates searchable 360° jobsite records for progress tracking and remote collaboration.

- **[HammerTech](https://www.hammertech.com/)**  
  Construction safety, quality, and compliance platform used for field inspections, incident reporting, and site documentation.

- **[Rhumbix](https://www.rhumbix.com/)**  
  Field productivity and time-tracking platform focused on labor data capture and production insights for construction crews.

- **[Touchplan](https://www.touchplan.io/)**  
  Collaborative planning and look-ahead scheduling platform for construction teams using pull-planning and constraint management methods.

- **[SiteMax](https://www.sitemaxsystems.com/)**  
  Construction operations platform covering field documentation, safety, equipment, and project controls.

- **[Assignar](https://www.assignar.com/)**  
  Workforce and equipment management platform for construction and field service—scheduling, compliance, and resource allocation.

- **[Fluix](https://www.fluix.io/)**  
  Document workflow and form automation platform used on construction sites for checklists, permits, and field paperwork.

## Open-Source GitHub Projects
- **[OpenProject](https://github.com/opf/openproject)**  
  Mature open-source project management platform with work packages, Gantt charts, and collaboration features that can be adapted for construction field coordination.

- **[ERPNext Construction module](https://github.com/frappe/erpnext)**  
  Open-source ERP with construction-oriented project, estimating, and operations capabilities that support broader jobsite and office workflows.

- **[Odoo Project + Field Service / community construction add-ons](https://github.com/odoo/odoo)**  
  Modular open ERP components for projects, tasks, inventory, and field service that can be configured for construction site management.

- **[FieldOpt and experimental field-management prototypes](https://github.com/)**  
  Early open-source efforts focused on job organization, technician/crew assignment, and location tracking for field operations.

- **[Redmine and issue-tracker open tools](https://github.com/redmine/redmine)**  
  Flexible open issue and project trackers usable for RFIs, punch items, and change-order style workflows.

- **[ProjectLibre / Gantt open desktop tools](https://github.com/)**  
  Open scheduling and Gantt alternatives useful for look-ahead and baseline planning on smaller projects.

- **[Document and form open workflow engines](https://github.com/)**  
  Self-hosted form and checklist tools that can replace paper field paperwork and simple inspections.

- **[Photo and progress documentation open collectors](https://github.com/)**  
  Community tools for capturing and organizing jobsite photos and daily logs.

- **[Time and attendance open mobile apps](https://github.com/)**  
  Open time-tracking components that can support crew attendance and labor capture on site.

- **[Documentation and construction PM open playbooks](https://github.com/)**  
  Guides for adapting general open project tools to construction field processes.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options
- Using **OpenProject** or **ERPNext** as a core for tasks, schedules, and document control when teams can invest in configuration.
- Combining open project tools with shared plan viewers and mobile forms for lightweight field coordination.
- Accepting that polished mobile plan markup, reality capture, industry-standard daily reports, safety compliance suites, and large-project scale still favor commercial platforms (Fieldwire, Raken, Procore, OpenSpace, HammerTech, Rhumbix, Touchplan, etc.).
- Focusing open-source efforts on data ownership, lower cost for smaller crews, and integration with open ERP systems.

**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Manage tasks and schedules in OpenProject or ERPNext → share drawings via open or commercial viewers → capture daily reports and punch items with mobile forms → store photos and documents in controlled repositories → optionally layer commercial tools for advanced field UX or reality capture. Suitable for smaller contractors and internal tool teams. Most mid-to-large construction firms rely on commercial field platforms for adoption and jobsite reliability.
